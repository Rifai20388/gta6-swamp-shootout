## The moment (decision pending — see bottom)

GTA 6 launched 19 Nov 2026. Launch week's viral story is the game's day-one
physics glitches, above all the "flying gator": an alligator launched into the
sky by the RAGE 9 physics engine, nicknamed the "Grassrivers Space Program"
(25M+ aggregate views on X/TikTok in 6 hours, per launch-week coverage).
Other viral day-one moments: a player falling through the map on the first
mission, an invisible-deadlift gym glitch, a Venetian Causeway guardrail
trampoline, a player crouch-walking with a shake in each hand.

## Moment choice — DECIDED

**The swamp shootout.** Jason swims back to the Grassrivers dock and gets
into a close-quarters gunfight with the hostile locals — cut from the Manny
Tomahawk Twitch VOD, 4:50–5:35 (45 seconds, one continuous moment).

Why this one, over the IGN first mission:
- The IGN video (scanned, 1 frame / 15 s, 60 frames) is a slow boat ride,
  binocular scoping, stealth takedowns, and a seaplane flight in a storm.
  Beautiful first look at Leonida, but no tension, no punchline — it would
  not stop a thumb.
- The swamp shootout opens cold on a local aiming a gun at Jason at the
  water's edge, then a pistol fight from inside the shack window, with the
  streamer's live reaction in the audio. Tension in frame one, action all
  the way through, daylight so it reads on a phone screen.
- A denser scan (1 frame / 5 s, 3:00–7:25) showed the video dies twice
  ("WASTED" ~4:10 and ~7:10), but the second death sits in dark interior
  footage with a pause menu in between — visually muddy. The daylight
  shootout window is the readable cut; the WASTED payoff is not worth the
  dark 2 minutes.
- No alligator is visible in this VOD despite the title — the "escape" is
  from the locals, not gators. The flying-gator moment is real launch-week
  news but exists only as other people's finished clips, which are not
  usable as source. This cut is cut by me from raw VOD footage.
